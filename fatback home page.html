BeerSteps
Submitting to the App Store

We like walking. We also like beer. BeerSteps is the fitness app that answers the one question your other apps never thought to ask: how many beers did I earn today? It connects to Apple Health and pulls your step data straight from your iPhone and Apple Watch, so your count is always accurate. From there, the math is simple — steps become calories, calories become beers. Pick your beer, set your goal, start walking.

Track your way

Earn beers all day long, or only during a set walk or hike — start a session when you head out, end it when you're back, and get a full breakdown of steps, distance, elevation, time, and calories.

Your beer, your rules

Not all beers are created equal. Choose from a range of styles, each with its own calorie target — the heavier the beer, the more steps it takes to earn it.

History that matters

Every session is saved. Review past walks and hikes with full stats, track daily step history, and watch your beer earnings add up over time — delete individually or all at once.

Syncs with Apple Health

BeerSteps reads directly from HealthKit, staying in sync with your Apple Watch and other health devices. Walk without your phone — your steps still count.
